Numeracy Co-requisite

Since 2024, every NCEA student — Level 1, 2 or 3 — has to pass the Numeracy co-requisite to be awarded NCEA at all. It's separate from your normal 60 credits, it's digital, and it's re-sittable. This page is built around the real CAA outcomes, so you can practise the actual format, not just the topics.
